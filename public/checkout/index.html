Frequently asked questions

What is RRU Rental?

A managed digital rental inquiry capture and qualification system that converts prospective renter inquiries into structured Renter Intelligence Briefs for human agent follow-up.

Does RRU Rental approve or deny renters?

No. RRU Rental does not make final rental decisions or approve or deny applicants.

How long does deployment take?

Your system is targeted for deployment within 72 hours after receiving your required business and onboarding information.
